These are notes from the author of this game.

Please dont get confused on this one note.
    The game modifies the currently loaded character or NPC. Only one can be loaded at a time,
    basically this means ED is looking at the file for that character at that moment in time.

    so if you want to change certain things about one character and already have one loaded,
    unload the current character to load the next.

Stat roles are 4 D6 roles and the lowest of your D6 roles is removed from the total count
    I.e. roll 3|6|5|2 total count is 3+6+5 which equals 14.

    currency is rolled via 2D20 + 1D8 and multiplying that by 10
    I.e roll 18|11|5 total count is 18+11+5 which equals 34 * 10 is 340. that is your currency.
